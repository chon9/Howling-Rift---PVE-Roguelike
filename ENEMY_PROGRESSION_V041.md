# Enemy comeback economy and level 100 — v0.4.1

Enemy bot heroes receive bonus gold beginning with their **eleventh death in a match**: 600 gold on death 11, 850 on death 12, 1,100 on death 13, then another 250 more for each subsequent death. This counts each enemy separately, including defeats by teammates or allied structures. Human players and friendly bots receive no death bonus. Duplicate death callbacks cannot award gold again.

Enemy bots spend the extra money through normal item prices, recipes and inventory limits. Their purchase tick now processes up to six affordable purchases, so accumulated gold turns into items quickly. After finishing their normal build they can spend excess gold on the existing escalating stat consumables. There are no free injected items. These gold rewards and counters reset with the match.

Enemy XP gains multiply by `1 + (N−1) × .02 + (N−1)² × .00015`. N1 remains ×1; N99 is approximately ×4.40. This applies to normal XP grants, including minion soak, last hits, champion kills and assists. It excludes players and friendly bots. Dead units cannot gain XP. The difficulty tooltip shows the multiplier; scoreboard level/gold tooltips show enemy XP and accumulated comeback gold.

All heroes can reach **level 100**. Levels 1–18 retain the existing XP costs and skill rank limits. Beyond 18, the next-level XP cost grows by 60 per level from the existing 1,880 threshold. There are still eighteen total skill points. Earlier unspent skill points remain available, but earning all eighteen stops new points from accumulating. Every subsequent level grants **+3 AD, +5 AP, +45 HP and +25 mana**, alongside the hero's normal per-level stat growth. A hero progressing from 18 to 100 receives 82 of these stat rewards. Skills do not acquire additional ranks.

Veteran level bonuses are separate from equipment, match shop items, consumables and roguelike choices. They stack, survive death/respawn and replicate to co-op clients; they reset next match. Level 100 discards excess XP and displays a full XP bar with a max-level tooltip. The existing level-up build choices still occur at 4/6/8/10/12/14/16/18.

Existing 81 PvE and 7,267 equipment checks passed. A host plus two local co-op clients completed without failures on the release engine. The actual exported AppID 480 Steam smoke test also passed with no failures, including enemy death-11 gold, level-19 stat rewards, equipment, respawn and daily drops.
