# v0.6.2 — Skill footprints

The aiming UI previously used a generic 90-unit circle for most point/target abilities, a generic 24-unit-wide corridor for lines, and an extra circle inside every cone. Active cone effects painted a complete circular impact behind the actual sector. Some directional kit effects used a fixed 60-unit width even when their damage checks used 52 or 84 units. Lucian's visual direction also followed the cursor rather than the selected target. Per-frame tight crops in the newer skill atlases changed visual scale across an animation. Tall pillars were centered using the whole image instead of their ground rim.

`SkillFootprint` now supplies circle, cone, capsule, rectangle, ring and pentagon geometry. Active ordinary AoE collision and its outline use the same shape. Circular damage areas with registered metadata share their radius with the preview. The boundary represents the area on the ground; collision still includes each target's existing body padding. Decorative glow, floating missiles and vertical pillars are distinct from the ground boundary. This does not rebalance damage, cooldowns, gold or XP.

Previews distinguish cast range from impact radius. Explicit sizes cover circular areas, cone sweeps, line widths, charge-growing Xerath Q, Yasuo's third Q, Syndra's thrown sphere, Brand's burning-target spread, each Jinx trap, Thresh Flay/Box/lantern, and Yasuo's wind wall. Targeted single-target spells and movement abilities use selection/destination markers instead of invented damage circles. Passive skills do not show a cast preview. Charged/secondary effects still happen according to their existing cast logic; a destination marker does not mean the dash damages that entire circle.

Active cones are clipped to their sector. Lucian Q uses the victim direction and a 26-unit half-width; Xerath Q uses a 30-unit half-width; Lancer's delayed thrust uses a 42-unit half-width and shows its wind-up. Thresh's Flay uses its 440-by-130 rectangle. Darius's pull and Syndra's push show their actual forward cone. Lux's laser and Yasuo's projectile-blocking wall show their existing ground dimensions. The spin aura reads the actual buff radius (72 for Bastion, 140 for Katarina) and uses the caster's colors.

Newer skill rows retain a consistent crop across their four animation frames; the bounded 48-row rectangle cache holds no textures. Ground profiles for Brand W, Leona R, Xerath W/R and Thresh R use stable original-cell anchors. A three-band mapping scales their painted ground rim independently from pillar height. Thresh's broken walls remove the corresponding decorative sector and ground segment. Existing Veigar ground-anchor mapping is preserved. This is a calibration of existing artwork, not a complete replacement of every hero's skill sheet; decorative hand-painted details can still differ from an ideal top-down outline.

AoE snapshots now carry footprint type and width along with their existing radius, angle, arc, direction and timers. All co-op players should update together; Steam protocol 7 and game-version compatibility reject older lobbies.

Validation includes real damage checks just inside/outside a circle, a rotated beam and a cone; warning delay; all 120 skill previews; stable/in-bounds atlas crops; reconstructed guest geometry; native rendering of every frame of all 72 expansion-kit skill effects and a visual gallery. A native three-player session checks beam, cone, Flay and broken-wall geometry on both guests. The full content audit, roster mechanics and combat interactions remain passing. Packaged export and updater checks are performed before release.
