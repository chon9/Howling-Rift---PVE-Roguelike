# Support art prompts — built-in image generation

All five assets were generated with the built-in image tool. Animation and effect sheets requested genuine transparency; icon atlases requested painted navy backgrounds. Atlas metadata and ground pivots were inspected in the generated output and in native gameplay.

## Seren actions — `assets/animations/heroes/soraka-actions-v1.png`

Use case: stylized-concept. Production MOBA sprite atlas on genuine transparent alpha. ONE original support heroine called Seren: celestial healer, ivory robe split for agile walking, teal hair, crescent-headed golden staff, emerald and pale gold magic. Mature detailed hand-painted isometric 2.5D game style. Exact SIX columns and TEN equal-height rows, every cell same dimensions, no grid lines, no text, no background, no cast shadows. Canvas portrait aspect 3:5, large enough for crisp sprites. Every cell contains one full-body identical heroine, facing RIGHT in three-quarter isometric view, feet at consistent baseline, scale and identity fixed, padding 12%. Each ROW is a six-frame progressive animation: row1 idle breathing; row2 full walk cycle; row3 staff basic attack windup then release; row4 Q staff thrust releasing a green star bolt; row5 W reach out to heal an ally with green palms; row6 E plant staff creating a purple silence spell; row7 R raise staff to call celestial healing light; row8 hit reaction recovery; row9 collapse death; row10 recall chanting. Precise ten rows, six columns, consistent registration, body entirely inside each tile. Props and small spell glows inside tiles. Clear changes between frames. No circles drawn on ground, no cross-cell effects, no duplicated limbs.
