# Howling Rift 0.2.7

- Owned item slots, shop cards, recipes and inventory use large gold-bordered hover panels with actual stats, passives and upgrade hints. Left-clicking a HUD item opens its direct and indirect upgrade paths. Clicking shop items selects them and browses their upgrades; finished items say that no further upgrades exist. Buy remains explicit; right-click inventory selling still requires shop access. The UPGRADES button provides the same view from item details.
- Expanded the level-up pool from 9 to 30 templates. Every three-option offer includes a role-specific template for tanks, casters, bruisers or marksmen, plus two varied options. Each offered stat rolls at 85–120% of its template amount, with 2.5% growth per level after level 4. Values shown are the exact values granted. Offers remain stable when reopening. Rewards still come exclusively from level milestones 4/6/8/10/12/14/16/18. Guest offers are rolled and granted by the host; exact stat values replicate. Bots choose role-oriented rolled upgrades too.
- Six frost-brush patches occupy the walkable north/south bridge edges. Hidden enemies disappear from view and the minimap, lose click/targeted-skill/autoattack targeting, and show HIDDEN in the team roster. Allies can see their own concealed heroes translucently. Entering the same patch, coming within 95 units, or allied tower vision reveals occupants. Attacking/casting reveals for two seconds; taking damage reveals for 1.25 seconds. Skillshots and areas can still hit concealed units. Bushes hide heroes, not minions. AI can flank and wait up to 2.5 seconds for an ambush, with cooldowns to prevent permanent camping. Healthy wave-supported tower pushes take priority.
- Bots notice visible non-homing hostile skillshots within 700 units and predict whether their line will collide. Reaction time uses the existing N curve (0.30 seconds at N1, about 0.087 at N99). They sidestep only when time and space remain, reject unsafe tower/fountain destinations, and cannot evade point-blank shots by instant reaction. Homing attacks do not trigger useless dodges.
- Legacy and expansion projectile sprite frames use precomputed per-row alpha bounds, with a shared crop across animation frames to prevent scale jitter. Mapping metadata avoids expensive pixel scans during gameplay. Spell effects get a restrained bright core. Warden/Thresh Q has a visible chain and one-second projectile lifetime. Rendering improvements do not enlarge collision radii.

Validation: TacticsUxTests verifies item click/hover behavior, recipe descendants, stable randomized offers, exact grant values, concealment/targeting/reveals, skillshots hitting brush occupants, hook/crop mapping, snapshot fields, reaction delays, impossible point-blank dodges, homing exclusions and safe ambush selection. Native screenshots cover the item upgrade view, hover tooltip and brush/hook presentation. Regression coverage includes three-peer LAN with exact guest rolled stats, lane simulation, existing kit/items, HUD and tower safety.
