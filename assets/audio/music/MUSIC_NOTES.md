# Original ambient score, revision 2

Oath of the Rift: 180-second menu/lobby ambient arrangement.
Bridge of Embers: 240-second lane ambient arrangement.

Replaces the previous busy 72/96 BPM synthesized string motifs with slowly changing minor/suspended harmony, sparse damped piano resonances and rounded pads. Battle percussion consists of two very quiet hits in occasional phrases, rather than a continuous drum pattern. No string ostinato, synthesized choir or bright repeating lead. Piano modes are slightly inharmonic with damped upper partials and softened attacks; these are locally synthesized instruments, not sampled orchestral recordings.

Original composition and synthesis in tools/create_rift_music.py. No external music, commercial recordings or samples. 48 kHz stereo Ogg Vorbis, with circular stereo reflections for loop continuity. MUSIC_REPORT.json records duration, source RMS/peaks and boundary delta. Listening sample: rift-music-preview.wav (menu then battle).

Crossfades take 2.4 seconds. Base music mix is 3 dB lower than the previous revision. New profiles default to 28 percent; existing saved user volumes are preserved. Announcer speech ducks music by another 6 dB, pause by 5 dB. Independent music slider still supports zero/off. The source itself is softer and sparser than revision 1.
